    About the team

    The Chemistry Football XI plays for the Department of Chemistry at Gopalganj Science & Technology University. We take on other departments in tournaments and friendlies, and train around lectures and lab hours.

    Players come from every year of the programme. If you can run, tackle or keep a clean sheet, we would like to see you at a trial. Ask the team captain or visit the department office.

    Cricket XI Season

    The team sheet in batting order. The first 11 players are the playing XI; everyone after them is on the bench (the first of them is the 12th man). The captain and vice-captain are chosen by the admin and can bat anywhere in the order.

      Admin: drag a row, or use the ▲ ▼ buttons, to change the batting order. It is saved for everyone. Use C and VC to pick the captain and vice-captain.
